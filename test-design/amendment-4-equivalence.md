## Amendment 5 (pacing)

Amendment 5 left every row of the table as it was at amendment 4. It changes pacing only: the 7 steps `wait 1 CommandsDispatched sample` of L0-2, L2-1, L2-2 and L7-1 become `wait 2 ticks` (`bench.wait(2.5)`, the form and duration of the plan's other two-tick waits). No command, marker, window, `require` or `check` changes.

Why the fixed wait cannot turn a failing check into a pass or hide a late sample. A wait sends nothing; samples come from the unit only, and every check is judged over the closed window from the record.

| test | check in `W` | effect of the fixed wait |
|---|---|---|
| L0-2 | at least 1 sample in the window | the end marker follows the command by 2 ticks; a sample later than that is outside the window and the check fails. The former wait admitted a sample up to 30 s late. |
| L2-1 | at least 2 rising samples, each step equal to the dispatches between them | each command is followed by 2 ticks before the next command or the end marker; a sample that is late or missing shortens the series or breaks a step, judged by the check. With packet 60 also held (planted fault) no sample arrives and the check fails on sample count, without a 30 s timeout. |
| L2-2 | same as L2-1, through packet 61 | same as L2-1 with the packets exchanged. |
| L7-1 | same check with both packets released | same as L2-1; with the final packets held (planted fault) no sample arrives and the check fails on sample count. |

## Amendment 6 (the check judges a sample after each dispatch)

Review finding `check-gap:last-update-not-judged` on amendment 5: with the sample wait gone, a sample missing after a command was no longer detected, because the start marker's own sample kept the count at two.

- Check, 3 rows marked "reworded: strengthened" (window `W` of L2-1, L2-2, L7-1). The name gains `, a sample within 2 ticks after each dispatch`; the helper is called with `within_ticks=2`. The added rule in `planted_faults.py`: with `end` the last dispatch row of the window (the end marker's), every dispatch `t <= end - 2 ticks` needs a sample with `t <= time <= t + 2 ticks`. The former rule (count, rising steps equal to the dispatches between samples) is kept whole and runs first.
- `fails` sets: the three planted faults that name this check carry the new name; the sets keep their shape (one entry each). `faults-menu.json` changes in those 3 lines only.
- L0-2 keeps its check unchanged (`min_samples=1`, `within_ticks` not passed, so the added rule is off). Its name states "at least 1 sample in the window" and no finding was raised on it; the added rule would hold there on the records in hand, but applying it would change a second check's name and meaning.
- Pacing: the 7 waits of amendment 5 become `wait 3 ticks` (`bench.wait(3.5)`), one tick more than the bound the check judges, so the live run and the replay see the same samples. The setup and teardown waits stay `wait 2 ticks`.
- Known-answer tests (`test/kat/test_helper_kat.py` beside each deployment, no bench): pass for the normal 3 samples and for a sample exactly 2 ticks after its dispatch; fail for the second command's sample lost, the first command's sample lost (merged step of 2), and a sample more than 2 ticks late. Each failing case also asserts that the former rule alone passes it.
